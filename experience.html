2021–2022 | PCR Diagnostics

First steps outside university

My first jobs after university took me into SARS-CoV-2 diagnostics, working with sample processing, nucleic acid extraction, RT-PCR and result review.

2022–2025 | CureVac

Process Development Technician

This is where the big shift happened: from running experiments to helping develop a completely new automated mRNA-LNP manufacturing platform.

I joined the project when the platform was still very much in development, so there was plenty to figure out. My work ranged from running development and pilot-scale studies to working on automated process sequences, analysing data and troubleshooting things that did not behave as expected.

Over time, the focus increasingly shifted towards getting the platform ready for GMP manufacturing. That meant qualification activities, SOPs, operator training and eventually supporting PQ runs. Somewhere along the way, I learned that getting a process to work and getting it ready for GMP manufacturing are two very different challenges.

2025–Today | CureVac

Process Development Engineer

My role gradually shifted towards designing and evaluating process characterisation studies, investigating unexpected results and taking more ownership of process understanding.

My role sits somewhere between experimental science, process engineering and detective work. I design and evaluate process characterisation studies, dig into process and analytical data, and investigate results that do not behave quite as expected.

A particular focus has become filtration, especially TFF and BBR. As SME, I support troubleshooting, process optimisation and technical investigations, often working across development, analytics and manufacturing when the answer needs more than one perspective.

Probably my favourite part is the moment when a messy dataset or unexpected result finally starts to make sense.
